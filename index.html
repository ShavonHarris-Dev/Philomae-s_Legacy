Philomae's Legacy

This reunion honors the memory and enduring legacy of our beloved matriarch, Philomae. Her strength, wisdom, and love laid the foundation for our family, and her spirit continues to guide us across generations.

🌟 Faith & Family

Philomae taught us that family is everything, and faith sustains us through all seasons of life.

💪🏾 Strength & Resilience

Her perseverance through challenges inspires us to overcome obstacles with grace and determination.

❤️ Love & Unity

She showed us that love binds us together, and unity makes us stronger than we ever could be alone.

As we gather in Memphis, we celebrate not just where we've been, but where we're going—together. This reunion is more than an event; it's a commitment to keeping Philomae's legacy alive for the next generation.
